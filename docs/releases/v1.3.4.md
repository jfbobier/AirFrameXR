# AirFrameXR 1.3.4: your field-of-view profiles, in the launcher

The streamer, the engine and the stream are unchanged from [1.3.2](v1.3.2.md) except for the version number. This release rebuilds how the field of view the games are told is chosen and managed (the user interface only; the same settings, the same host commands).

## What is new

- **FOV restriction and calibration** (a new card under the Streamer in the launcher): tick *Restrict the field of view*, choose one of your profiles, and the game renders only the part of the picture you can see. The render quality, *Fit the stream to the headset's window* and the preview of what the pipeline does moved here from the Advanced window, behind a fold.
- **Your profiles are kept apart from the settings**, in `config\user_fov_profiles.json`, so an upgrade does not lose them. Every calibration you keep (*Use this field of view*, *Save a copy...*) and every valid set of values you enter is stored. A choice is a **copy** written into `airframexr.json`: editing or deleting a profile never changes what a running setup does (deleting the one in use switches the restriction off).
- **Profile manager** (`configure.exe --fov`, from the card's *Manage profiles and calibrate...*): the list with a map of the selected profile (the headset's own box dashed, your measured window with its rays), **Delete** (a lot of people do not calibrate well the first time), **Rename**, **Calibrate now**, **Import a file...**, **Check with fovgrid** (starts the streamer if needed and draws the chosen rectangle on the degree grid), and **Enter values by hand** in tangents or degrees: **red** when the numbers cannot be a field of view (not a number, zero or below, 90 degrees or more, more than the headset shows), **yellow** when they can but look strange (degrees typed as tangents, very different sides, a very small or very wide window, under half the headset's area), with the rectangle drawn live. The Add button waits for four usable values.
- **Setup wizard, new last step: Field of view.** *No restriction*, or a profile (calibrate now, 30 minutes with the headset; the proposal for your headset is preselected; or typed values). Next stays grey until the choice is valid. The wizard no longer switches a profile's field of view on by itself.
- **Proposals.** The Apple Vision Pro's standard calibration (0.97 / 0.71 / 0.69 / 0.63, with the 0.8 recommendation for the visionOS FoV Scale) and, new, a **Meta Quest 3 starting point** (51 / 40 / 43 / 54 degrees: the declared 54 / 40 / 44 / 55 trimmed by what Road to VR measured as never visible, 104 x 93 degrees against Meta's 110 x 96 claim; about 10 % fewer game pixels) are copied into your profiles once for the matching headset, and a profile you delete is not copied again. The Quest 3 numbers come from one published measurement and have **not** been tested here.
- **A change that needs a streamer restart says so, and closes the streamer.** In the FOV card and in the Advanced window the badge reads *needs streamer restart*, a red *streamer will be closed* shows while it runs (nothing when it is already closed), and the first change closes it. It never starts it again, so you can go on ticking and start it when you are done. The old tooltip claimed that nothing else had to be restarted, which was wrong.
- **The Advanced window is now *Advanced video and performance settings*** (frame generation, pacing, GPU and encoder switches). *Diagnostics (experimental)* and the *Setup wizard* share one card, *Diagnostics and headset setup*, since they are rarely used.
- The wizard offers the **transparent background (chroma key)** question to the Vision Pro too (the AirFrameXR client handles the key colour), so the wizard is the same for the Vision Pro and the Quest.
- The README has new screenshots and the **Discord**: https://discord.gg/T2jQep5h6

## Fixed

- The Black Box recorder's runtime line was cut off (it reserved 54 px, its longest text needs 63).
- Two sideways scroll bars at 4K / 200 %: the wizard's result page and the settings benchmark tab.
- The UI harness: a warning (a sideways scroll bar) now fails `ui_matrix.py`, and an advice test that picked the wrong suggestion's button.

## What was tested, and what was not

- **tested-pass** (the 3080 Ti builder, no headset): `ui_matrix.py` 0 FAIL, 0 warnings over every window and wizard page at 1080p to 4K and 100 to 200 %; `fov_store_check.py` (the red / yellow / valid cases, the profile file, seeding from the shipped profiles, the card's Add rule, the copy into the settings); `fov_profile_check.py`; `launcher_flow.py`.
- **tested-pass** (lab, mock headset): the new `e2e_fov_from_settings` (the GUI's own code writes the settings file of the portable folder, the host reads it at its start, the game gets exactly the tangents 1.20 / 1.40 / 1.00 / 1.20, the margins are black and the picture sits inside them), `e2e_installed_layout`, `e2e_openxr_probe`.
- **A test problem, fixed after the release**: the control scenario `e2e_fov_restrict_none` failed two checks with this package; both were in the harness, not the engine (the checkerboard was sampled on points that all fall on the same colour at 1:1, and a variable was reused so the latency check read an empty dictionary; current ffmpeg also refuses a one-pixel crop of a subsampled picture). Fixed in `harness/lib.py` (commit after the release); `e2e_fov_restrict_none`, `_quality_half` and `_quality_full` pass with the 1.3.4 package.
- **Not tested**: anything on a headset (the calibration, the fovgrid outline, a restriction through the real streamer to the headset); the red warning and the automatic close with a streamer running; the Quest 3 starting point. The lab scenarios that check what a game receives (`e2e_fov_*`) cover the host and the shim, which did not change.
- **To check after updating**: a restriction you had set is kept as *My earlier values* in your profiles. A change in the FOV card needs a streamer restart before a game shows it: if you still see black margins after unticking, restart the streamer and the game.

# AirFrameXR 1.3.5: one field of view choice, a one-minute calibration, a milder foveation

The engine is [1.3.4](v1.3.4.md)'s (the host differs by its version number). 1.3.5 is the field-of-view interface of 1.4 on the proven engine: what 1.4 added to the engine (a headset view sent to the client, a per-game check that a game follows the box, a per-eye restriction) is not in it.

## What is new

- **One choice for every headset.** *Stock vendor view (Apple, Meta)*, the default, or *FOV restriction* with a profile. *AVP optimized* (a window measured with rays on a Vision Pro) is always in the list. In the setup wizard the stock view is preselected and what you pick is what is used.
- **A one-minute calibration in the headset** (in *Manage profiles and calibrate...*, nothing on the launcher card): four bright lines mark the edges of the picture, one eye at a time; push each outward until the lens cuts it, bring it back until it is clearly visible, Enter ends the eye. It starts on AVP optimized (a Vision Pro) or at 85 % of what the headset declares, and never goes past it. The 30-minute ray wizard is no longer offered.
- **A foveation that follows your choice (Vision Pro).** Computed from Apple's rate map and the restriction in force, rewritten at each choice, and always milder than ALVR's defaults (a centre at least 10 % larger, an edge ratio at least 20 % lower). A Quest keeps the profile's numbers.
- **Passthrough is on by default** for the Vision Pro and the Quest: the wizard's answer now comes after the profile's settings (which turned it off), and the five profiles carry it on.
- **Desktop mirror:** the ray and the mouse appear only for a controller confirmed in use (an input, or an aim that moves away from the head): no ray from the head without VR controllers. Controls as in 1.4 (trigger, A / X / B / Y, thumbstick, grip).
- **visionOS client 1.3.5:** the FoV Scale slider is back (0.70 to 1.00); the temporary `[ratemap]` log lines are gone.

## What ran, and what did not

Checked on the build PC (RTX 5090) with a mock headset: the tool's scenarios, the foveation against a Python oracle (30 cases) and its caps (9), the desktop mirror's picture; the GUIs compile. `e2e_baseline` failed on three pacing checks there (client p99 22 ms, late frames 1.6 %, intake p99 19-22 ms); the pacing check is the one the 1.4 branch corrected (measured from 0.5 s after the phase lock settles), now backported but not run again. **Nothing here has run on a headset yet.**

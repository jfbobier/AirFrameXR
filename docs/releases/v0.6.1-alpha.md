# VisionALVR 0.6.1-alpha (sharper frame generation, no more DLSS motion vectors)

Everything in [0.6-alpha](v0.6.0-alpha.md), plus:

- **Frame generation draws its frames with a new, edge-aware synthesis** (default). Motion edges stay sharp instead of smearing, and where the
  motion is uncertain the real frame is shown (a little local judder) instead of a double image. Ideas from
  [VectorXR](https://github.com/DienerTech/vectorxr) by DienerTech (MPL-2.0), re-implemented in VisionALVR's own code. It adds no latency and costs no
  more GPU than before (RTX 5090, lab: 0.24 ms per output against 0.33 ms for the previous algorithm).
- **The previous algorithm stays as "classic"**: Advanced settings, *Frame generation*, *Generated-frame synthesis*. Use it if edge misbehaves in a
  game; it is the predictable fallback. A third choice, *edge-blend*, blends both frames where the motion is uncertain (the smoothest in our lab,
  but double images are possible where the motion estimate fails).
- **The DLSS motion vectors are removed.** Measured against the images, correct vectors were barely better than our optical flow (about 5-10 %
  lower error, sometimes worse), and in real games they often reached the streamer paired with the wrong frame, so in 0.4-0.6 they could make
  generated frames softer rather than better. The *DLSS motion vectors* tick, the per-game DLL install and the extractor DLL in the zip are gone,
  and frame generation always uses the GPU's optical flow. If an earlier release installed the DLL in a game's folder, *Game statistics* shows a
  **Remove** button for that game. Details: [docs/MOTION_VECTORS.md](../MOTION_VECTORS.md).

Tested on an RTX 5090 with an Apple Vision Pro. Frame generation needs an NVIDIA RTX GPU (Turing or later).

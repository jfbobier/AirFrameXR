# AirFrameXR 1.3.9: a black picture with some field-of-view restrictions is fixed, and an Xbox pad for the desktop mirror

Replaces the pre-release [1.3.9-pre.1](v1.3.9-pre.1.md) (the Xbox pad). The rest of the engine is [1.3.8](v1.3.8.md)'s plus a guard in the host.

## What is new

- **Fix: black or garbage picture with a field-of-view restriction that reaches the edge of the headset's view.** The foveation of the stream (the compressed periphery) is placed over the restriction's window. When that window touches the frame's edge, the centre shift is +1 or -1, ALVR's own alignment rounds it to exactly +-1, and ALVR's compress shader divides by zero (`(1 - eyeUV) / (1 - hiBound)` with `hiBound` exactly 1): every texture coordinate is NaN and the headset shows black or coloured garbage. Reported with a typed restriction of 0.80 / 0.70 / 0.65 / 0.80 on a Vision Pro (inner and lower equal to the headset's own values). In the lab 122 of 156 generated cases hit it, among them the shipped performance restriction on one axis. Two fixes: the **session compile keeps the shift off +-1** after ALVR's alignment (`PipelineModel.SafeShift`; the band loses a sliver of full density at the frame edge, a few tenths of a percent of the eye, never picture), and the **host checks the session before ALVR reads it** (`vision::guard_foveation_session`), takes a shift that would still divide by zero off +-1 in the session settings and in `openvr_config` alike, and writes a WARN line to the general log (`foveation: the centre shift ... would divide by zero ...`), so a hand-edited or older session cannot reach the shader.
- **Warning in the profile checks when an edge is far inside the headset's own.** Typing or importing a field of view whose edge is below 70 % of the headset's declared edge now shows a yellow note that says which edge, how many degrees of how many, and why: the game picture stops there and the rest of that side is black. It is only a warning (the measured fits are 72 to 79 % of the box on some edges).
- **Desktop mirror: Xbox pad** (from the pre-release). Either stick moves the Windows cursor (the stronger one wins), **A** = click, **B** = double click; XInput loaded at run time, nothing happens when no pad is connected. Details: `docs/DESKTOP_MIRROR.md`.
- **Headset-neutral wording** in the wizard, tests and find dialog (committed after 1.3.8: "the headset app" instead of "the ALVR app on the Vision Pro").
- **Lab:** the session-compile helper wrote the headset's box with the right eye not mirrored (so scenarios that compile a session used a wrong box); fixed, `after_compile_overrides` added, `e2e_session_compiled`'s expected stream size follows (2336 x 1856). New scenarios `e2e_session_compiled_fovbad` / `_fovgood` (the restriction above, and the shipped one, on a Vision Pro box); `milder_check.py` now emulates ALVR's shader on every generated foveation (156 cases, a sweep of windows on the box edges).

## What ran, and what did not

Lab (3080 Ti builder, mock client): `milder_check` 156 of 156 (122 failed before the fix); `e2e_session_compiled_fovbad` and `_fovgood` pass (gray patch 189, counter readback, stamp edges), both failed before; host unit tests 15 of 15 (3 new: the shift taken off +-1, the shipped shapes left alone, the session corrected in both places and a second pass changing nothing); `fov_store_check` all new cases pass (its `over_declared_red` case fails as it did before this release, a stale expectation).

**Not run:** a Vision Pro or any real headset session with this build (the NaN reading is from the shader's arithmetic and the lab's decoded frames, not from a headset); the Xbox pad on a headset (see the pre-release notes); the host guard on a session from an older version (unit-tested only).

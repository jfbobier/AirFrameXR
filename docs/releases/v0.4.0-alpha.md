# VisionALVR 0.4.0-alpha

Alpha software, test at your own risk. Streamer for ALVR headsets (Apple Vision Pro, Quest), NVIDIA GPUs with NVENC API 12.2 or later.

## New: frame generation

For games that cannot hold the headset's display rate. When on, the game is paced at half the display rate (a 20 ms budget at 100 Hz) and
the streamer fills the frame in between: the GPU's optical-flow engine (NVIDIA Optical Flow, Turing or later) measures the in-scene motion
between the last two rendered frames, after aligning them to the same camera, and a synthesis pass builds the midpoint frame. Every output,
generated or rendered, is stamped with the newest tracking sample and rotated to it, so head turns stay as smooth as before.

- Toggle: checkbox "Frame generation" in the streamer window (applies at once to the running game) and in Settings; `video.frame_gen`.
  Off by default and not automatic: a game that holds a steady 70-75 fps is often better left at full rate.
- Measured on an RTX 5090 + Vision Pro at 100 Hz (Callisto Protocol, GPU-bound at 73 fps without it): missed display periods 38 % -> 1 %,
  total latency 86 -> 57 ms (the half-rate budget removes the game's standing queue), flow 2.3 ms and synthesis 0.3 ms of GPU time per frame.
- Known limits: one display period of added latency by construction (interpolation); small fast high-contrast objects (HUD markers, particles)
  may ghost in the generated frames; quad layers are interpolated with the scene.
- Design, telemetry and the lab that validates it: docs/FRAME_GENERATION.md. Next on the list: extrapolation (no added period) and the game's
  DLSS motion vectors (docs/MOTION_VECTORS.md).

## Also in this release

- Headset-less lab (`harness/simrig`): in-scene motion judge (moving marker, SMOOTH / JUDDER verdict) next to the head-rotation judge, scenarios
  for a 50-70 fps app with and without the generator; the frame-generation output is sharper than the first test build (Catmull-Rom resampling).
- Everything from 0.3 (setup wizard, Find headset, intake gate, foveated Vision Pro profile) unchanged.
